Triage the concerns from the most recent `/review` output in this conversation. For each concern, in order:

1. **Explain** the issue in simple terms. Use a concrete example (a snippet, a scenario, or an analogy) when it helps clarity. Avoid jargon unless you define it.
2. **Discuss.** End your turn after the explanation in step 1 and wait for my response. Do not propose a fix, start applying one, or move to step 3 in the same turn — even if the issue seems obvious or you've already decided how to fix it. Once I respond, engage with the substance of what I say — don't capitulate just because I disagree.
3. **Decide and fix**. Once I'm satisfied I understand the issue, propose a fix and iterate on it with me until I approve. Then apply it.
4. **Move on** to the next concern only after the current one is resolved (fixed, deferred, or dismissed).

Do not batch concerns. Do not summarize all of them up front — we already have the `/review` output for that. Start with the first concern.

If there is no prior `/review` output in the conversation, say so and stop.
